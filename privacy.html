Privacy — Midnight Felt

Last updated 12 November 2026.

The daily run can be ranked by an optional online service. This page says exactly what that service stores, what it never stores, and how to get your data back or delete it. If you play offline, or open the single-file build, nothing leaves your browser.

What we store

  • A random player id and secret. When you first create a player, the server generates a pseudonymous id and a secret. The secret is shown to you once and kept in your browser. The server stores only a SHA-256 hash of it. Neither value is linked to your name, email, or any account, because there is no account.
  • A display name, only if you set one. Names are optional, limited to 3–16 characters, and shown only to friends you accept.
  • Your daily starts and results. The day key, when you started, and the outcome the server computes by replaying your actions: rounds cleared, score, win or loss, and when it was recorded.
  • A coarse score bucket. Results go into an aggregate histogram (day, rounds, bucket, count) that produces the “better than X%” figure. The histogram has no player ids in it.
  • A fair-play flag. A result can be marked as reviewed when it looks automated. Flagged results are kept but left out of the histogram.

What we do not store

  • No email address, password, phone number, or real name.
  • No IP addresses in our database. Rate limits are counted briefly at Cloudflare’s edge (10–60 seconds) and are not persisted by us.
  • No cookies, no advertising or analytics trackers, no third-party profiling.
  • No action logs beyond the run you submit: they are replayed once and not stored.

Where data lives

The service runs on Cloudflare Workers and stores data in Cloudflare D1. Cloudflare processes requests on our behalf as a hosting provider. Your game saves, settings and player secret are kept in your own browser’s local storage; clearing site data removes them. The secret is sent only in an Authorization header, never in a URL.

Percentiles

The percentile is computed from aggregate counts only. Other players cannot see your results, and you cannot see theirs, except through the optional friends board, which shows a friend’s name and daily result only after you have both finished that day.

Fair play

For the ranked daily, the game engine runs in your browser. That means a determined player could simulate the day ahead of time and submit an optimal line. We reduce this with one ranked attempt per player per day, a server-side replay that must match, and review of suspiciously fast or extreme results. It is not used to identify anyone, and the percentile is meant to be a casual comparison.

Your choices

  • Export. The game can download everything stored about you (player id, name if set, and your results) as JSON.
  • Erase. Deleting your player removes the player row, every start and result, and subtracts your results from the histogram in one atomic step. This cannot be undone. To keep your streak statistics, the game also keeps a local copy in your browser; clear site data to remove that too.
  • Stay offline. Decline ranked play, or use the single-file build, and no data is sent anywhere.

Retention

Starts and results older than 400 days are deleted automatically by a daily job. Aggregate histogram rows survive only as counts for days that still have results.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and the online service has no accounts to create beyond a random player id.

Changes and contact

If this notice changes, the date at the top changes with it. Questions or requests about your data: privacy@example.com.
